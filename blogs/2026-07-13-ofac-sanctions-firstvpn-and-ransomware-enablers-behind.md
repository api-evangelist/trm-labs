On July 13, 2026, OFAC sanctioned the FirstVPN Service (1VPNS), its administrator Dmytro Rashevskyi, and cryptor vendor Yevgeniy Silayev — targeting the anonymity and malware-obfuscation services ransomware groups rely on rather than a ransomware group itself, with TRM data showing operators paying the service directly on-chain.
